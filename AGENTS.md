# AGENTS.md

Guidance for AI coding agents working with OpenCode.

## Writing style: hand the reader one idea at a time

Applies to everything you write — replies, commit messages, documentation, code
comments. Assume the reader is not holding the context: tired, mid-context-switch, or
arriving cold.

- **Restate the question in one line before answering.** Give the anchor rather than
  assuming it.
- **One idea per sentence, one point per paragraph**, two or three sentences per
  paragraph. A sentence with three subordinate clauses is three sentences wearing a coat.
- **Label options by what they mean, not by their technical name.** "Don't have several
  styles" beats "converge on a single house style".
- **Lead with the recommendation, then the plain reason.**
- **Keep cross-references out of the body.** A mid-sentence `§4` or "(see below)" is a
  lookup the reader must perform or feel guilty about skipping. Collect them at the end.
- **Bold the claim, not the keywords.** One bold sentence per section; scattered bold
  marks nothing.
- **One insight per reply, at the end.** An insight in every paragraph means none lands.

Serialising costs words, not substance: same content, arriving one piece at a time. Two
companion rules govern other axes — write in a calm, settled-fact voice rather than a
promotional one, and name the concrete behaviour rather than its abstract label.
